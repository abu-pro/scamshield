      How ScamShield works

      1 · You paste it

      Any text — SMS, chat, email — or a bare link. No account, no upload of personal data.

      2 · Shield reads like a fraud officer

      Thirteen red-flag families — urgency, credential asks, fake prizes, look-alike domains, untraceable payments — are checked one by one, and every link is taken apart piece by piece.

      3 · You get a score and a plan

      A 0–100 risk score, each flag explained in plain English, and the exact steps to stay safe — or to recover if you already acted.

      The red-flag library — learn the patterns once, spot them forever

      ⏰ The rush

      "Within 24 hours", "final warning", "act now". Pressure exists to stop you checking.

      🔑 The ask

      Passwords, PINs, OTPs. No real organisation asks. Ever. The ask is the scam.

      🎁 The prize

      You won a lottery you never entered — just pay a small fee first. The fee is the whole business.

      🔗 The look-alike

      paypaI-secure.xyz is not PayPal. One different letter, a strange ending — your savings gone.

      💸 The payment

      Gift cards, crypto, wire transfers. Untraceable on purpose, unrecoverable by design.

      🤫 The secret

      "Don't tell anyone, stay on the line." Isolation is a weapon — a second opinion kills most scams.
